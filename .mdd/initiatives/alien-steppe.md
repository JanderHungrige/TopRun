# Alien Steppe

## Overview
Owner (2026-11-14): *"Lass uns eine neue welt generieren. Mit allem drum und drann. Intro, medallien, uniform, skin,
arseenal, forschung ... Machen wir einen mix aus arcade und tower defence. Die neue welt ist eine steppenwelt mit aliens.
Leg mal los"*

Map 7 (m181–m210) after the jungle's finale (m180): the fleet lands on **Kharsa**, a steppe planet an alien army is
overrunning. The pilot drives a fleet of **tracked tanks and combat buggies**. The missions **alternate** between the
arcade drive the game knows (the steppe scrolls, waves, objectives) and **tower defence**: hold an outpost at the
bottom of the screen against alien columns marching down their lanes, build and upgrade towers on build pads with the
supply the kills bring, and fight beside them. Everything a world has: an intro, a launch game, medals, a ceremony
in steppe uniform, a celebration and tape, a top rank, paint jobs, an arsenal, a research lab, the base's pictures.

## Decisions (question round, 2026-11-14)
- **Vehicles:** tracked tanks AND combat buggies, the fourth fleet kit (`tank`) beside helicopters, subs and mechs —
  bought with **money** like the helicopters and subs (no alloy). The free **Jackal** buggy (E nitro dash), the
  **Lynx** light tank (E sentry drop), the **Scorpion** rocket buggy (E rocket salvo) and the **Bison** heavy tank
  (E main-gun shell). Tanks swivel their turret toward the target, buggies bounce and drift; tracks and tyres leave
  marks, dust trails behind.
- **Arcade and tower defence alternate:** in each theater m1, m3, m5 are arcade drives, m2, m4, m6 tower defence;
  the boss mission (m6) is a defence: the boss marches on the outpost after the last wave.
- **Tower defence:** an outpost with a health bar at the bottom; aliens march down 1–3 lanes drawn on the steppe, fliers
  head straight for it; build pads beside the lanes. Drive onto a pad to build or upgrade (BUILD key / tap); towers are
  paid with **supply** from kills, a start budget and a bonus per wave; a countdown between waves (call the next wave
  early for a bonus). The outpost falling or the vehicle dying loses the sortie. Towers: MG nest (known from the start),
  flak tower, mortar pit, tesla tower, jammer (slows), rocket battery.
- **Research like the jungle lab:** research points per sortie, one project at a time; the lab invents the tank
  weapons, the tower types and their marks (a tower's mark is the highest level it can be built to in a mission). The
  lab's level (money) sets the points per sortie. **Everything else costs money**: vehicles, the turret cannon, its
  upgrades and ammo (the arsenal, like docs 99 and 101), equipment, the lab's level.
- **Aliens: an invasion army** marching in columns — crawlers, alien troopers, beast riders, tripods with heat rays
  (a heat ray overheats a tower for a few seconds), siege crawlers, shield bearers, brood carriers, landing pods; scout
  drones, disc fighters, bomber mantas; the WAR TRIPOD mini-boss; bosses that are colossi and motherships: HARVESTER,
  COLOSSUS, BEHEMOTH, DREADNOUGHT and the MOTHERSHIP.
- **Theaters:** Sea of Grass (m181–m186), Salt Flats (m187–m192), Red Badlands (m193–m198), Barrow Fields
  (m199–m204), the Crater (m205–m210, finale).
- **Launch: the ramp start** — the lander's ramp drops, the lights count down; keep the revs in the green band and
  release the brake on green (rated like the catapult).
- **People:** Captain Rosa "Bastion" Ortega commands the outposts and calls the waves; Dr. Ilya "Sparks" Novak runs the
  steppe lab (new generated faces); the chief, Shooter and Duke get steppe outfits.
- **Honours:** seven medals for map 7, a steppe ceremony (the pilot and the admiral in steppe camouflage), a
  celebration, a tape, the new top rank.
- **Skins:** paint jobs for the tanks and buggies, earned like the jets' (doc 100's way).
- **Deploy:** the world goes live once, after the last wave (a half-built world stays off the live site).
