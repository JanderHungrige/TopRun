# Deep Water

## Overview
Owner (2026-11-07): *"I want another world. An underwater world. With bubbles and appropriate weapons and enemies.
For this world and the space world we also need a new hangar look. Space and underwater dock."* Map 5 goes under the
sea: it opens after m120, the wallet carries, the kit starts fresh (like maps 3 and 4), and the player steers a
**fleet of attack submarines**. The home base gets new looks for the space world and the underwater world.

Decided with the owner (one question round, 2026-11-07):
- **A fleet of 3–4 subs** (like the helicopters, doc 51): a free starter sub and faster / heavier / stealthier models
  to buy, each with its own special, and upgrades in the shop.
- **Military plus sea monsters:** enemy subs, sea drones, mines, seabed turrets and torpedo boats on the surface,
  and creatures — giant squid, eels, jellyfish swarms, a sea-serpent boss. Player weapons: a rapid harpoon gun,
  homing torpedoes, depth mines for the seabed, a sonar burst (the starter sub's E), supercavitating rockets.
- **A full map** like the others: 30 missions in 5 theaters with bosses, a launch minigame (the dive from the
  carrier's well deck), its 7 medals and its celebration.
- **Dock looks:** the owner wants to see generated home-base examples (space-station dock, underwater dock) before
  deciding between generated backdrops and code-drawn ones.
