# Home Base Honours

## Overview
Rewards you can show off at the home base (owner request): **medals** for campaign milestones, combat
feats, minigame golds and dedication, each awarded in a short **pixel-art ceremony** (the admiral pins
it on); a **locker** where a large pixel-art **dress-whites** uniform shows every ribbon and medal (tap
one for its citation); and a **picture wall** with one generated photo per cleared mission — your jet
in the paint job you flew, over that theater, with stars and date. The BASE hub becomes the home base
with three rooms: Rec Room (minigames), Locker, Picture Wall. Generic award names only (public site:
no real decorations' names or designs).
