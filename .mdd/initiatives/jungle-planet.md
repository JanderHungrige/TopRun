# Jungle Planet

## Overview
Owner (2026-11-08, before going to bed): *"Now for the next world, we land on a jungle planet. We have mechwarriors as
machines. Walking around. But now we have to build up energy generator, weapon production, … This earns us resources
to build mechs. To build extensions and upgrades. For some weapons like rockets, we have to build them to have x ammo.
Also new weapons have to be invented not just bought. These are two new areas that can be reached from the hangar
image, the ground base and the lab. Next to this new mechanic, we need the usual images for the cosy base, the loadout
items, loadout background, new person images, mech warriors, ribbons, ceremony scenes, etc. Enable also painting your
mechs jungle camo yourself. We also need new mini games. Actually for all new worlds. At least for the underwater,
space and now jungle world. Use the M1. The other is unfortunately offline. Go fully ahead with everything, as I need
to go to bed. Go through all waves and deploy. I will check tomorrow."*

No question round (the owner was going to bed and said to go fully ahead); the decisions below are Claude's, written
down so the owner can change them.

## Decisions
- **Map 6, the Jungle Planet** (m151–m180), after space's finale (m150). Five theaters: Landing Zone, Rainforest
  Canopy, Mangrove Swamp, Temple Ruins, the Hive. Enemies are machines and creatures, like the sea: walkers, hover
  tanks, artillery, gun drones, dropships; raptors, armoured beetles, spitter plants, wasp swarms; a heavy walker mini-boss
  (BRUTE); bosses RAVAGER, MANTIS, BASILISK, SENTINEL and the HIVE QUEEN.
- **Mechs, a fleet of four walking machines** (a third fleet kit beside the helicopters and the subs): the free
  Strider (jump jets), the Ranger (missile barrage), the Hornet (camo field) and the Titan (bulwark). They walk: a gait
  that surges with every step, legs that stride, footprints in the mud, the world scrolls at a walking pace.
- **Alloy instead of money in the mech kit.** Mechs, autocannon levels and equipment are *built* in the MECH BAY from
  alloy; the money (the one wallet of every world) pays for the **buildings** of the GROUND BASE.
- **The ground base:** a generator (power), a refinery (alloy), a munitions factory (builds ammo), the lab (research)
  and the mech bay (builds mechs and upgrades), each to level 3. Power is a grid: the generator's output against what
  the other buildings draw; short of power, the refinery and the lab produce less. Production runs **per sortie** on
  map 6 (each mission flown is a day at the base), plus salvage from wrecks.
- **Built ammo:** the rocket pod, seeker missiles and the mortar fire rounds from the base's magazine, which the
  factory fills (alloy per round; the factory's level sets the magazine). A sortie takes what fits on the racks and the
  rounds fired are gone. Energy weapons (plasma lance, rail cannon) need no rounds.
- **The lab invents weapons:** seeker missiles, the mortar, the plasma lance and the rail cannon, and the Mk II / Mk III
  of every mech weapon, are research projects; the lab adds its research points to the active project after every
  sortie. Nothing in the mech kit is bought with money.
- **Two new areas in the hangar image:** the jungle base's crew hut (a generated picture, cozy like the space dock) has
  the ground base (a window onto the compound) and the lab (a door with a green-lit window) beside the usual areas.
- **People:** Major "Dozer" Brennan runs the ground base, Dr. Ada "Flask" Moreau the lab (new generated portraits); the
  chief and Shooter wear jungle outfits; Shooter calls the drop.
- **The drop:** map-6 sorties start with a drop from the dropship: fire the retro rockets at the right height (rated
  like the catapult).
- **Camo:** the PAINT SHOP in the mech bay: paint each mech's armour pixel by pixel in jungle colours, with stamps
  (blotch, stripe, tiger), fill and a random camo; the mission's mech wears exactly that paint.
- **Honours:** seven medals for map 6, a jungle-clearing ceremony, a celebration, a tape for the TV, a top rank.
- **New minigames, one per new world:** SONAR HUNT (under the sea), STATION DOCKING (space), FIRING RANGE (jungle).
