# New Frontiers

## Overview
Owner (2026-11-06): *"we need a third and fourth stage."* **Map 3** is flown with **helicopters instead of
jets** (Apache, Comanche, Eurocopter Tiger, Hind); the jets are not available there and the loadout starts from
scratch. Some map-3 missions run through a **mountain-gorge obstacle course** ("Hindernisparcours"): canyon walls
close in while the enemies keep coming, and touching a wall crashes the helicopter. **Map 4 goes to space**: again a
fresh kit with new weapon types, and only **one spacecraft that evolves** — the pilot buys modules (a new front
capsule, a wing for rockets, …) and **places them on a grid** on the ship, which changes its shape. Plus an
**admin pilot**: choosing the 4th preset pilot and naming him ADMIN unlocks every mission and gives unlimited money,
to try things in the game.

Decided with the owner (one question round, 2026-11-06):
- Plan as one initiative, **admin pilot first** so every later wave can be tried in the game at once.
- **One wallet, fresh kit:** money carries into maps 3 and 4; the jets (and their kit) stay with maps 1–2; map 3
  opens a helicopter hangar and map 4 a spacecraft hangar where everything starts at level 0 (one starter
  helicopter and the bare spacecraft are free). Prices are set so the money a pilot brings is a head start, not a
  maxed kit.
- **Shape is the hitbox:** every module adds power but also size and mass — a bigger ship is a bigger target and
  turns slower, so each build is a trade-off.
- **New launch games:** the helicopter lifts off the deck in gusting wind; the spacecraft rides a rocket and the
  pilot times the stage separations. Both are rated like the catapult (doc 04).
