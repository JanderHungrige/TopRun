# Second Front

## Overview
Owner (2026-10-27): the first campaign is played through too fast. A **second map with another 30 missions**,
clearly harder, reached through a **small globe** on the campaign map. Map 1 stays untouched. On map 2 the hangar
grows: **three new jets** — the A-10 Warthog (E: a mega minigun with a very high rate of fire and the iconic A-10
cannon sound), the F-35 (E: invisible, enemies do not attack meanwhile) and the Tornado (a challenge for hardcore
pilots: it is so old it slowly loses hull during every mission; E: a nuclear missile). The new jets get **double
firepower** — every weapon twice, e.g. a second minigun next to the level-3 one that levels up on its own, twin
rocket pods; the old three jets get **more upgrade levels per weapon** and a **stronger air-to-air and
air-to-ground weapon**. All of it only shows up in the hangar once map 2 is reached. Plus **new camos** for the new
jets and new ones to unlock in general.
